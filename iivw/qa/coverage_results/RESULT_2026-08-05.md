## Note on block calibration

`validation_iivw_inference.do` records a calibration of 89.7 min for a 50-rep
`iiw` block "on an idle box" — that measurement is from a **16-vCPU host** and
does not transfer to this machine. Measured here under ~30-way concurrency:
`iptw` ≈ 40 min, `iiw` ≈ 95–128 min, `fiptiw` ≈ 140 min per 25-rep block. Note
also that this host's 28 vCPUs are SMT siblings of **16 physical cores**, so
aggregate throughput is ~16–20 single-thread-equivalents, not 28.
