# Two-step (stacked) sandwich — arithmetic proof and calibration probe

**Diagnostic, not a gate.** Nothing here may be relabelled as a coverage verdict
or used to change a default (`FIPTIW_NSCALE_2026-07-23.md:5-7`,
`_take_action/se_recovery.md` §11). No default was changed. `vce(stacked)` ships
**off by default** and stamped `e(iivw_inference_status)` =

## What is computed

Per-subject influence contribution, in unnormalized form:

```
psi_i = D^-1 [ U_i + G A^-1 s_i ]

D    = sum_j w_j v(mu_j) x_j x_j'                      (canonical-link bread)
U_i  = sum_{j in i} w_j x_j (y_j - mu_j)               (outcome score)
G    = sum_j w_j (y_j - mu_j) x_j (dlog w_j/dtheta)'   (cross-derivative)
A^-1 = block-diagonal inverse information of the nuisance fits
s_i  = subject i's stacked nuisance score
```

Nuisance parameters propagated: the visit-intensity Cox `gamma`, the propensity
logit `alpha` and its intercept, and the IPTW stabilization numerator
`p = Pr(A=1)`. The stabilization model's `delta` is deliberately absent: B&L show
the outcome estimating equation has mean zero for **any** `h` (PDF p.9), so its
derivative in `delta` vanishes at the truth and the first-stage estimation of
`delta` has no first-order effect — which their p.11 also states directly.

## Two things that could not be transcribed and had to be derived

**1. The papers' displayed formula is not directly implementable.** B&L's `V̂`
carries a minus in front of an `Ĥ` that already absorbs the minus from
differentiating `exp(-gamma'Z)`, and their `Ĥ` display omits the `1/n` that `D̂`
and `Â` both carry. Read literally it gives either the wrong sign or the wrong
scale. Coulombe's Newey–McFadden form settles both: with `M = E(grad_phi m)` the
*negative* information, `-G_phi M^-1` is `+G A^-1`. The implemented sign is
**plus**.

**2. The shipped weight is not the weight the papers differentiate.**
`iivw_weight` renormalizes the IIW component to **mean 1 over the modeled
events**, and that divisor is a function of `gamma`. So

```
d log iw_j / d gamma = -(Z_j - Zbar_omega)
```

the deviation of `Z_j` from the **weight-weighted** mean of `Z` over the modeled
events — not the papers' `-Z_j`. Dropping the second term is not a rescaling of
the correction; it is a different vector, and it does not vanish as `n` grows,
because the estimator is invariant to a common factor on the weights while
`-Z_j` alone is not. Study-entry rows pinned at exactly 1 have derivative 0.

Which rows are modeled events is knowable only while the weights are being
built. That is why `scores` is an option on the **weighting** and the fit refuses
rather than reconstructing.

## The arithmetic is exact, and that is checked three ways

Every input is Stata's own object, and each is pinned against the robust variance
Stata itself would report from it (`test_iivw_stacked.do`):

| what | identity | worst reldif |
|---|---|---:|
| S1 Cox stage | unrobust `stcox` `e(V)` + per-subject score sums vs `stcox, vce(cluster id)` | **4.3e-19** |
| S2 logit stage | unrobust `logit` `e(V)` + scores vs `logit, vce(robust)` | **1.7e-17** |
| S3 bread | the helper's own fixed sandwich vs `glm [pw=], vce(cluster)` | **1.6e-16** |

S3 is also a **gate**: `iivw_fit` recomputes the fixed sandwich from the same
bread and residuals it uses for the stacked one and refuses to post the stacked
matrix unless it reproduces the fitted covariance to 1e-8. A wrong bread errors
instead of silently shifting the correction. S4 shows that zeroing every
`dlog w/dtheta` collapses stacked onto fixed **exactly** (1.5e-16), and S5 that
the term is applied on a real fit (1.8e-3) — the pair matters because the
correction is small in the mean for this estimator, so a coverage-style check
has no power to notice it being dropped.

**S14 pins the renormalization term**, which is the one line of the derivation
neither paper states. It reconstructs `-(Z_j - Zbar_omega)` independently from
the shipped weight and the covariate and matched to **0.0e+00**, with a guard
that the comparison is not vacuous: the weight-weighted mean of `Z` was 0.293
against an unweighted 0.181, so the term the papers omit is materially nonzero
in the fixture.

None of that establishes the correction has the right **size**: every identity
above would survive `G` being scaled by a constant. That is what the probe is
for.

## Calibration probe — the correction is right where the asymptotics apply

IPTW, correctly specified propensity model, truth 0.5. Mean SE against the
empirical SD of `beta-hat`, and the realized coverage of the nominal 95% Wald
interval. Every row below is the output of `qa/probe_stacked_calibration.do`
with the invocation shown, so each is regenerable from the repository.

| cell | invocation | reps | SD / IQR-scale | fixed SE/empSD | fixed cov | stacked SE/empSD | **stacked cov** |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mild, `n=400` | `probe 250` | 250 | **1.011** | 2.285 | **1.000** | **0.988** | **0.944** |
| strong, `n=400` | `probe 250` | 250 | 1.369 | 1.177 | 0.976 | 0.705 | 0.832 |
| strong, `n=1600` | `probe 150 770000 1600` | 150 | 1.205 | 1.210 | 0.967 | 0.793 | 0.880 |

**The mild cell is the decisive one.** Its `SD/IQR-scale` of **1.011** says the
sampling distribution really is near-normal, so the empirical SD is a fair
target. There, treating the estimated weights as known makes the interval
**2.28x too wide and 100% covering**, and the stacked sandwich lands within
**1.2%** of the empirical SD at **94.4%** coverage.

That is the textbook estimated-propensity result — treating the propensity score
as known is conservative for the ATE — reproduced end to end, and it confirms the
sign and the magnitude of the correction *together*. Neither is separately
checkable by the identities above: a wrong sign would make the stacked interval
**wider** than the fixed one, and a `G` scaled by a constant would miss the
empirical SD by exactly that constant. Only this measurement rules both out.

**The strong cells are a different failure, and it is not the correction's.**
`SD/IQR-scale` rises to **1.369** at `n=400`: under positivity strain the
sampling distribution is heavy-tailed, so the empirical SD is inflated by a
handful of replications and is no longer a fair target. Against the robust scale
instead, the stacked SE is **3.5%** low at `n=400` (0.965) and **4.5%** low at
`n=1600` (0.955) — not 30% and 21%. The residual gap is a **tail** the asymptotic
variance structurally cannot see; no variance estimator repairs a heavy-tailed
pivot. The fixed sandwich's better coverage there (0.976 and 0.967) is
over-coverage that happens to land near nominal, not accuracy.

Raising `n` from 400 to 1600 in that cell moves stacked coverage 0.832 to 0.880
and the ratio 0.705 to 0.793 — the right direction, and slowly.

This is the same shape as `_take_action/se_recovery.md` §12's unresolved 16%
scale deficit: an analytic variance running low against an empirical spread that
weight strain has made heavy-tailed. It is the first time this package has seen
both phenomena in one experiment.

## What this does not establish

- **Nothing is cleared.** No candidate passed anything. §11's clauses were not
  reached and are not claimed.
- **No coverage evidence exists at the gate's DGP, cell, or seeds.** The probe
  is IPTW at `n=400`/`n=1600` on its own seeds. It is not the FIPTIW `n=300`
  preregistered cell and must not be reported as one.
- **The FIPTIW default is unchanged** and remains point-only.
- **The strong-confounding gap is open.** Quadrupling `n` from 400 to 1600 moved
  the ratio only 0.705 to 0.793 and coverage 0.832 to 0.880. Whether that is slow
  convergence under positivity strain or something the correction misses is not
  established; the robust-scale reading says most of the gap is the tail, but
  "most" is not "all" and 150 reps is a screen, not a result.
- **Scope is refused, not approximated.** `model(gee)` and canonical links only
  (`gaussian`/`identity`, `poisson`/`log`, `binomial`/`logit`); weight trimming
  is refused outright, because a weight clipped at an estimated quantile of
  itself is not differentiable in the nuisance parameters.

## Reproducing

```bash
# from an isolated scratch copy preserving the repo layout
cd <scratch>/iivw/qa
stata-mp -b do test_iivw_stacked.do                 # the arithmetic, 13 cases
stata-mp -b do probe_stacked_calibration.do 250            # mild + strong at n=400
stata-mp -b do probe_stacked_calibration.do 150 770000 1600  # the n-scaling row
```

The probe is excluded from every standard lane (`_skip.txt`) for the same reason
`probe_cr_ladder.do` is: it is an on-demand instrument, not a curated pass/fail
suite.
